# 🏠 Ghar Jaldi Aa! – Home Run

A fun, fast endless-runner for mobile and desktop. Dodge Indian street traffic and reach home before Mummy-Papa lose their patience!

**▶ Play now:** https://aisolutionservices.github.io/Ghar-jaldi-aa/

Built by [AI Solution Services](https://aisolutionservices.in). Available in **Hindi** and **English**.

## How to play
Run 1000 m to reach home before the timer ends.

| Obstacle / Item | What to do |
|---|---|
| 🛺 Rickshaw, 🐄 Cow | Change lane to dodge. A hit costs 1 life and 4 seconds |
| ⚫ Pit | Jump over it |
| ⚠ Wire | Slide under it |
| ⏱️ Coin | +10 score. Every 12 coins gives +1 life (if hearts are not full) |
| 🔑 Key | 5-second speed boost; obstacles can't hurt you |
| 🗺️ Map | Shortcut: 150 m closer to home |

## Controls
- **Touch:** swipe ← → to change lane, ↑ to jump, ↓ to slide
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys or A / D / W / S (Space = jump), **P** or **Esc** = pause
- **⏸ button** (top-left) pauses the game; the game also pauses automatically when you switch apps or tabs

## Features
- 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard) and 5 levels of rising traffic
- Boy or girl runner
- Hindi / English language switch
- Mummy-Papa phone calls with voice, plus a heart-warming ending scene
- Best score saved on your device
- Game-over summary: score, distance, coins, levels completed and best score
- Separate volume sliders for music, sound effects and voice
