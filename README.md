# 🏠 Ghar Jaldi Aa! – Home Run

A fun, fast endless-runner for mobile and desktop. Dodge Indian street traffic and reach home before Mummy-Papa lose their patience!

**▶ Play now:** https://aisolutionservices.github.io/Ghar-jaldi-aa/

Built by [AI Solution Services](https://aisolutionservices.in). Available in **Hindi** and **English**.

## How to play
Run 1000 m to reach home before the timer ends.

| Obstacle / Item | What to do |
|---|---|
| 🛺 Rickshaw, 🐄 Cow | Change lane to dodge. A hit costs 1 life and 4 seconds |
| ⚫ Pit | Jump over it |
| ⚠ Wire | Slide under it |
| ⏱️ Coin | +10 score. Every 12 coins gives +1 life (if hearts are not full) |
| 🔑 Key | 5-second speed boost; obstacles can't hurt you |
| 🗺️ Map | Shortcut: 150 m closer to home |

## Controls
- **Mouse (desktop):** drag/swipe on the game area, or click the on-screen ◀ ▲ ▼ ▶ buttons
- **I** key (or the ℹ button) announces the current status
- **Touch:** swipe ← → to change lane, ↑ to jump, ↓ to slide
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys or A / D / W / S (Space = jump), **P** or **Esc** = pause
- **⏸ button** (top-left) pauses the game; the game also pauses automatically when you switch apps or tabs

## Features
- 3 difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard) and 5 levels of rising traffic
- Boy or girl runner
- Hindi / English language switch
- Mummy-Papa phone calls with voice, plus a heart-warming ending scene
- Best score and a top-5 leaderboard saved **only on your device (not online)**
- 🎓 Practice mode: a short guided run with no timer or life loss that names each obstacle as it appears, and ends with a practice summary (dodged, hits, coins, power-ups, time)
- ⚙ Settings & Help screen: how to play, volume, on-screen controls, voice test
- Five level themes (sunset, day, evening, dusk, starry night)
- Game-over summary: score, distance, coins, levels, time used, hits, obstacles dodged, power-ups and best score
- Separate volume sliders for music, sound effects and voice

## Accessibility
- Screen-reader announcements (status every few seconds, upcoming obstacles with lane, hits and level-ups) through a live region outside the canvas
- Keyboard-focusable on-screen controls (◀ ▲ ▼ ▶), on by default on desktop, switchable in Settings & Help
- Lane changes, jumps, coin counts and the **I** status key are announced; Settings & Help shows these instructions
- Mouse drag-swipe works on the game area; dialogs have focus management and visible focus outlines
- The page language (`lang`) switches with the Hindi/English toggle

## Voice compatibility
Voice uses the browser's built-in speech engine. Settings & Help lists how many Hindi/English voices the device has, which female/male voice was picked, and has **Mummy** / **Papa** test buttons. Captions always show on screen if a voice is missing.

Please test on: Chrome on Android, Samsung Internet, Safari on iPhone, Chrome and Edge on desktop. For each: open Settings & Help, press both test buttons in Hindi and in English, and note any missing or unclear voice.
